FIRST, THE BIG PICTURE

What does Bouncer actually do?

Think of it as a purchasing assistant for an agent. You set the boundaries; Bouncer helps the agent get the resources on its list without spending outside those rules.

01You set the plan

Choose the resources, allowed payees, tokens and spending limits.

02The agent gets quotes

It requests and checks quotes against your prepared instructions.

03Bouncer checks & pays

A NEAR contract enforces the token, payee and spending rules.

04The service delivers

After verifying payment, the development provider fulfills the request.

i Today, requests are prepared by a person. Bouncer is not yet an AI planner or an open marketplace.

THE BOUNCER RULEBOOK

Built to say no.

The agent can ask to spend. The on-chain policy decides whether that payment is allowed.

Owner-only policy changesChanging rules needs exactly 1 yoctoNEAR. A function-call key can’t attach it.

Caps that follow every paymentPer-transaction limits and a conservative rolling daily budget.

Approved payees onlyUnknown payees and ungranted agent keys are rejected.

HONESTLY, WHERE WE ARE

Working today. Building toward more.

      Compute + data + public storage
      
The Bouncer vault enforces the reviewed caps and allowlist. Storage is public NEAR contract state, not an independent storage provider; results and original hashes may be public. Never put secrets in storage.

      
Your wallet signs an approval for the displayed mission or single step, authorizing the configured server's restricted agent keys.

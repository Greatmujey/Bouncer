# OutLayer testnet diagnostic status

This is an experimental route toward one independently operated **compute diagnostic**, not evidence of commercial delivery or the complete multi-resource product. OutLayer testnet advertises the fake token `usdc.fakes.testnet` (USDC.e), **not Circle test USDC**. The reviewed `connector-probe` burn is priced at 10,000 fake atoms and needs a separate bounded test-NEAR deposit. A provider prepayment may be returned without updating V11's tracked ledger; recovery and deployed-source equivalence remain unverified. The V11 vault is unaudited.

On 2 November 2026 the existing vault's Circle balance was 6,006,907 atoms in both the token contract and Bouncer's ledger; its existing Circle cap and grant were unchanged. The [read-only OutLayer preflight](https://github.com/Greatmujey/Bouncer/actions/runs/41541915355) found the fixed diagnostic registration stage eligible using distinct newly generated public keys. The keys are held locally in two authenticated encrypted copies, with the wrapping key also local; they are **not installed** and no off-device backup is confirmed.

The original fake-token registration [workflow](https://github.com/Greatmujey/Bouncer/actions/runs/41542420534) could not safely confirm transaction `7Rce6FLjRJ18GtUWcVgReZkMGCaTvvisXB93WWvGTFkV`. Two read-only RPC lookups did not return its original receipt; that is not proof of historical absence. **Never resend the original.** A later, distinct zero-yocto control [`Fegksi5b8KmqnHN3fHaH1Ak9vyLFA2JjgoqL5UAtjpu2`](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/Fegksi5b8KmqnHN3fHaH1Ak9vyLFA2JjgoqL5UAtjpu2) deliberately triggered the owner-policy 1-yocto guard and finalized with a higher owner-key nonce. This fences any later acceptance of the original registration but does not prove whether it had run earlier. A fresh FINAL view after the control reported both fake-token registrations absent. The [control workflow](https://github.com/Greatmujey/Bouncer/actions/runs/41543580190) retained a public original-hash fence and verified the expected guard rejection. **Never resend the control either.**

No OutLayer prepayment, order or paid execution has been made through this path. Any new registration must first verify the **original control's FINAL receipt**, its original-hash binding and fresh absence; it must use its own durable original hash and one-shot checks. Do not interpret a test failure, a current balance or this documentation as a paid-provider result.

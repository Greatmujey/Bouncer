Why this needs a contract. A NEAR function-call key's allowance is a gas budget, not a spending limit — and it can't be lowered once issued (only deleted). Gas is near-free, and such a key can still call ft_transfer, so a “scoped” key can drain the whole balance. Policy-mutating methods here require 1 yoctoNEAR, which function-call keys cannot attach, so a compromised agent key is structurally locked out of widening its own policy.
